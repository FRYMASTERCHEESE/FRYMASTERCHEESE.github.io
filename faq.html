Detailed FAQ

Detailed answers about gameplay, accounts, referrals, progress, reward records, security and advertising.

Is Tap Rush free to play?

Yes. The browser challenge itself does not require a purchase.

How long is one round?

Ten seconds.

Are points worth dollars?

No. Points are gameplay progress and do not automatically convert into money.

What does Best mean?

It is the highest single Tap Rush result recorded for the player.

Can I use an auto-clicker?

No. Tap Rush is intended as a manual skill challenge.

What does a pending referral mean?

The referral relationship was recorded but has not been treated as verified.

Does a referral guarantee payment?

No. Referral records do not automatically create a monetary balance.

Does the site currently send crypto or PayPal?

No. The current site records requests but does not itself execute those payments.

Should I provide a seed phrase?

Never. A private key or seed phrase should remain secret.

Do ad clicks earn game progress?

No. Advertising is separate from gameplay and rewards.
