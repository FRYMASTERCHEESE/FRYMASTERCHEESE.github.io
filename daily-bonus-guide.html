Daily Progress Bonus Guide

What the daily bonus does, when it resets and why it remains a game-progress feature rather than cash.

What the bonus is

The daily progress bonus is an optional game feature that adds a fixed amount of points and XP. It gives returning players a small progress marker even on days when they do not chase a new Tap Rush best.

How often it can be used

The interface records the date of the most recent daily claim and prevents another claim for the same date.

What it is not

The bonus is not a cash payment, cryptocurrency distribution or guaranteed financial reward. It changes game progress only.

Why keep it simple?

A predictable daily feature is easier to understand than a random prize mechanic. Players know what the action changes and do not need to interpret odds or hidden reward rules.
