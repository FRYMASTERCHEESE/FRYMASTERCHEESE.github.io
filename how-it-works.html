How FryMasterCheese Works

A complete walkthrough of Tap Rush, progress saving, referrals, daily progress and separate reward records.

1. Play a short skill round

Tap Rush uses a ten-second timer. After Start is pressed, manual taps on the cheese target increase the round counter until time expires.

2. Record progress

A completed round adds its tap total to points, adds XP and may update the personal-best result. The daily progress bonus can add a separate fixed amount once per day.

3. Use an account for supported cloud progress

Players can create an account and sign in. Supported progress can then be associated with that account rather than existing only in the current browser.

4. Understand referrals

A new account can supply an existing player's referral code. A referral can have a status such as pending or verified. Duplicate accounts, self-referrals and manipulated sign-ups are not genuine referral activity.

5. Keep game progress separate from reward records

Points, XP, levels and personal bests describe gameplay. Pending, verified and paid USD records are separate. A withdrawal request can only use a separately verified balance.

6. Know the current limitation

The current site records withdrawal requests but does not itself send PayPal or cryptocurrency payments. A secure real-money system would require server-side verification, fraud controls and a payment execution process.
