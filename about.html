About FryMasterCheese

Why this independent browser-game project exists and how its progress system is designed.

A small skill-game project

FryMasterCheese is built around short, understandable browser challenges. Tap Rush gives players ten seconds to record manual taps and compare the result with their own previous best.

Why we publish guides

Rules and terminology should be understandable. The site documents points, XP, levels, referrals, safety and reward records instead of leaving players to guess what numbers mean.

Progress and rewards are separate

Browser-side scores can be altered or automated. Ordinary game statistics therefore are not automatic proof of monetary entitlement. Any reward record requires separate verification.
