How it works

1. Play

Tap Rush is a ten-second reaction challenge. Tap the cheese as many times as possible before the timer ends.

2. Build progress

Points, XP, levels and best streaks describe game progress. They are not cash or cryptocurrency.

3. Save an account

Email authentication can associate supported progress with your account.

4. Referrals

A new account can enter an existing player's referral code. Self-referrals, duplicates and artificial registrations do not qualify.

5. Reward verification

Reward-related activity must pass applicable verification before an amount becomes verified. A client-submitted score alone is not proof of monetary entitlement.

6. Withdrawals

Only an available verified balance can be requested. A request enters processing and does not itself send money.
