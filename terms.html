Terms of Use

Fair-play, account and reward-record terms for FryMasterCheese.

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Fair use

Do not use automation, scripts, modified clients, duplicate accounts or other methods intended to falsify gameplay, referrals or reward eligibility.

Game statistics

Points, XP, levels and best results do not by themselves represent cash, cryptocurrency, stored value or a guaranteed reward.

Reward records

Only separately verified amounts may be eligible for a withdrawal request. The current website records requests but does not itself execute payments.

Accounts and security

Protect login credentials. Never provide a private key or seed phrase. Check any public payout destination carefully.

Advertising

Advertising is separate from gameplay and reward eligibility. Users must not be asked or incentivised to click advertisements.

Changes

Features and documentation may change for maintenance, security, abuse prevention or development. Meaningful project changes are recorded on the Updates page.
