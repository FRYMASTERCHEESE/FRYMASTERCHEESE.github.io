Points, XP & Levels

What each FryMasterCheese progress statistic means and how reward records are kept separate.

Points

Points are a running gameplay statistic. Completed Tap Rush rounds add their tap total; the optional daily bonus adds game-only progress.

XP and levels

XP measures continued game activity and is used to calculate the displayed level. Neither XP nor a level represents financial value.

Best result

Best records the strongest Tap Rush round, while points are cumulative. They intentionally measure different things.

Reward records

Pending, verified and paid are separate record types. Game points and XP do not automatically create a monetary balance.
