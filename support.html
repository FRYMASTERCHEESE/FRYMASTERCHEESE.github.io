Support & FAQ

Answers about Tap Rush, accounts, progress, referrals, reward records and advertising.

How long is a round?

Ten seconds.

Why are points not cash?

They are gameplay statistics. Browser-generated values can be manipulated, so they are deliberately separate from monetary verification.

Why might a referral be pending?

Referral records can remain pending while genuine activity and duplicate-account concerns are checked.

Does the site send PayPal or crypto payments?

No. The current website records requests only; it does not contain a server-side payment sender.

Should I enter my seed phrase?

No. Never provide a seed phrase or private key.

Do ads affect rewards?

No. Advertising is separate from gameplay and users must never be required or encouraged to click ads.
