Account & Wallet Safety

Practical guidance for passwords, email recovery, public crypto addresses, private keys and seed phrases.

Use a unique password

A unique password limits damage if another service you use experiences a breach. Protect the email account connected to your login because it may be used for confirmation or recovery.

Public addresses are not private keys

A cryptocurrency receiving address is intended to identify where funds should go. A private key or seed phrase controls the wallet itself. Those secrets should never be entered into FryMasterCheese.

Watch for impersonation

A message claiming you must reveal a seed phrase, private key or password to receive a payout is unsafe. Legitimate payout processing does not require possession of your wallet's secret recovery information.

Check destinations carefully

Blockchain transfers can be difficult or impossible to reverse. Network and address details should be checked carefully before any real payment is sent.

Current site capability

The website can record a withdrawal request against a separately verified balance. It does not currently contain the secure server-side payment worker required to execute PayPal or cryptocurrency payouts.
