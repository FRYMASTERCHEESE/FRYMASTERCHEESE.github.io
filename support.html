I did not receive a confirmation email

Why are points not cash?

Points are game progress. Monetary rewards use a separate verification process.

What does pending mean?

Pending means the activity or request has not completed applicable checks.

Can I submit my seed phrase?

No. Never enter a seed phrase or private key.

Why is a referral pending?

Referral activity can require checks for genuine activity and duplicate accounts.

Advertising and rewards

Advertising is separate from gameplay. Players are never required or encouraged to click ads for points, XP or rewards.
