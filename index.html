Project design

Skill first

Tap Rush is intentionally simple: a ten-second manual challenge with a personal-best target.

Progress is not cash

Points, XP and levels describe game activity. They remain separate from reward records because client-side values are not sufficient proof of monetary entitlement.

Clear safety information

Public wallet addresses and private wallet secrets are different. Private keys and seed phrases should never be entered into this site.
