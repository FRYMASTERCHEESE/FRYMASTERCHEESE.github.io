Accessibility & Comfortable Play

Ways to make short Tap Rush sessions more comfortable and easier to use across different devices.

Use a comfortable setup

Position the screen so you do not need to strain your wrist, hand or neck. Short rounds are designed to be quick; there is no need to continue if tapping becomes uncomfortable.

Take breaks

Repeated fast tapping can become tiring. Rest between groups of attempts and stop if you experience discomfort.

Text and zoom

Modern browsers provide text-size and page-zoom controls. Increasing text size can make guides easier to read, although changing zoom may alter how much of the game area is visible at once.

Keyboard and assistive technology

Informational pages use ordinary headings, links and form labels to support common browser navigation patterns. The Tap Rush target is primarily a pointer/touch interaction and may not provide an equivalent competitive experience for every assistive input method yet.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is part of site maintenance rather than a one-time task. Interface changes should continue to consider readable contrast, understandable labels and comfortable mobile layouts.
