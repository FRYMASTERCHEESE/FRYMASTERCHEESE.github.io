Project Updates

A public changelog of meaningful gameplay, content, safety and account improvements.

7 October 2026 — V4 content expansion

Expanded the player resource library with strategy, daily progress, referrals, reward-record transparency, browser troubleshooting, accessibility, challenges and a detailed FAQ. The homepage now gives the educational content greater prominence and cross-links related resources.

7 October 2026 — V3 transparency update

Added the first dedicated Tap Rush, points/XP, fair-play and account-safety guides and improved the site's content navigation.

26 September 2026 — Account and policy pages

Added About, How It Works, Privacy, Terms and Support information and clearer separation between gameplay statistics and reward records.

Progress-saving improvements

Cloud loading includes points, XP, best result and reward-record fields, and save failures are surfaced instead of being silently ignored.

Development priorities

Useful gameplay, documentation, accessibility and stronger server-side verification remain priorities. Real payouts should not be represented as operational until a secure server-side payout process exists.
