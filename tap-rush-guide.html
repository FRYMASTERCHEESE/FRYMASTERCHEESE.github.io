Tap Rush: Complete Player Guide

Learn the rules, timing, scoring, personal-best system and practical ways to practise Tap Rush consistently.

RulesScoringPracticeFair play

The basic rules

A Tap Rush round lasts ten seconds. Press Start, then tap the cheese target manually until the timer finishes. Taps before the round begins or after it ends do not belong to the active round.

How scoring works

Your round score is the number of taps counted during the active timer. When a round finishes, that number is added to the points total. XP also increases, while Best keeps the highest single-round result recorded for the player.

Total points and Best are different

Total points accumulate over multiple rounds. Best is only the strongest individual round. This means a player can keep increasing total points even when a new round does not beat the personal best.

How to practise consistently

Use the same device when comparing sessions, place the device on a stable surface if that feels comfortable, and judge improvement over several rounds rather than one attempt. Touchscreen response, hand position and fatigue can all affect a short test.

Build accuracy before speed

Missing the target or changing hand position repeatedly can interrupt rhythm. Start with comfortable, deliberate taps, then increase pace while keeping the motion controlled.

Fair play

Tap Rush is designed for manual interaction. Auto-clickers, macros, scripts and modified clients replace the human action the challenge is meant to measure. See the fair-play guide for more detail.

Remember: a Tap Rush score is a gameplay statistic. It does not automatically create a monetary reward.
