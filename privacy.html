Privacy Policy

Privacy information for FryMasterCheese accounts, gameplay, referrals and reward-request records.

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Information used

Account features may process an email address, account identifier, referral information, gameplay progress and withdrawal-request information you choose to submit.

Purpose

Information supports authentication, cloud progress, referral records, account operation, abuse prevention and service maintenance.

Local and cloud data

The browser can store local gameplay state. Authenticated features may separately load supported account progress from the backend.

Service providers

Supabase supports authentication and backend data features. Google advertising services may be used on eligible publisher-content pages in the future, subject to applicable policies and consent requirements.

Advertising and gameplay

Advertising is separate from gameplay. Ad interactions do not earn points, XP, referrals or rewards.

Wallet safety

Never submit a cryptocurrency private key or seed phrase. A legitimate receiving destination only requires a public address where applicable.
