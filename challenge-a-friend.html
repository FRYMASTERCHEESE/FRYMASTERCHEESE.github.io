Challenge a Friend

Tap Rush is designed to be easy to share: play a real round, share the result and invite another person to try the same ten-second challenge.

How it works

  1. Open Tap Rush from the homepage.
  2. Complete one manual ten-second round.
  3. Use Share Score or Copy Challenge Link.
  4. Send the message to a friend or post it on your own social page.
  5. Your friend plays their own round and compares the result.

Keep sharing genuine

Do not use bots, traffic exchanges, automated refreshes or purchased fake visitors. A real person choosing to play is much more useful than an artificial page view.

Advertising stays separate

Players are never asked to click or view advertisements for points, XP, referrals or rewards. Sharing a game result is about the challenge itself.
