Fair-Play & Anti-Cheating Guide

Understand manual-play expectations, duplicate-account rules and why client-side scores need independent verification.

Manual input is the point

Tap Rush measures manual interaction during a short timer. Auto-clickers, macros and scripts can generate input unrelated to the player's own tapping and therefore undermine the challenge.

Modified clients are not trustworthy evidence

Browser code can be inspected and altered. Changing local values, calling functions outside normal gameplay or submitting fabricated data does not represent legitimate progress.

Referral integrity

Creating duplicate accounts, referring yourself through additional accounts or coordinating fake registrations makes referral counts misleading. Genuine referrals should represent distinct people choosing to join.

Why server verification is important

A future system that converts skill activity into real value would need server-authoritative sessions and anti-fraud checks rather than trusting a score supplied by the browser.

Ads are never a gameplay task

Advertising is separate from points, XP, referrals and rewards. Users must not be instructed, incentivised or rewarded to click or view Google ads.
