Account & Wallet Safety

Practical password and cryptocurrency-address safety for FryMasterCheese players.

Protect your login

Use a password you do not reuse elsewhere and protect the email account used for confirmation and recovery.

Public address versus private key

A public cryptocurrency receiving address can identify a destination. A private key or seed phrase controls the wallet and must remain secret.

Never enter a seed phrase here

FryMasterCheese does not need a seed phrase or private key for a withdrawal request.

Current payout capability

The current website can record requests against separately verified balances, but it does not itself execute PayPal or blockchain payments.
