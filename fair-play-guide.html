Fair-Play Guide

Why manual play, genuine accounts and separation between advertising and rewards matter.

Manual play

Tap Rush is intended to measure a person's manual interaction during a short timer. Auto-clickers, macros and scripts undermine that purpose.

Genuine accounts

Duplicate accounts and self-referrals make referral statistics unreliable and are not genuine participation.

Why verification matters

Browser values can be manipulated. A responsible monetary system cannot assume every client-submitted score is trustworthy; server-side verification is needed before gameplay could safely support real rewards.

Advertising is separate

Advertising, if enabled on eligible publisher-content pages in the future, is separate from gameplay. Players must never be asked or rewarded to click or view an advertisement.
