About

FryMasterCheese Play & Earn is an independent browser-game project focused on short skill challenges, progress tracking, referrals and clear reward records.

What players can do

Players can play Tap Rush, track points and XP, record a personal best and use supported account features. The aim is to keep gameplay simple while explaining what every statistic means.

Why gameplay and money are separate

Browser game data can be manipulated, so an ordinary score should not automatically create money. Gameplay statistics are separated from verified reward balances.

Fair play and security

Use normal device input and one genuine account. Automation, scripts, duplicate accounts and manipulated referrals can make activity ineligible. Never provide a cryptocurrency seed phrase or private key.
